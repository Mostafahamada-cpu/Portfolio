## CRM & Client View

A complete CRM system designed to help businesses manage clients, track interactions, and organize customer information in one place.

### CRM Dashboard

* Manage and organize all clients.
* View client details and contact information.
* Track client status and activity.
* Add, edit, and manage client records.
* Search and filter clients easily.
* View important customer data from a centralized dashboard.

### Client View

A dedicated client-facing interface that allows each client to access their own information and follow their requests or activities.

* Personalized client profile.
* View current status and updates.
* Access relevant information and services.
* Simple and responsive interface.
* Clear communication between the business and the client.

### Goal

The goal was to create a simple CRM experience for the business while providing clients with a clean and easy-to-use interface.
