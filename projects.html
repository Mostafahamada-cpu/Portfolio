RingRoad Attendance

Developer

I developed a web-based attendance management system designed for companies to manage employee attendance, working hours, leave requests, and attendance reports in one place.

Overview

I built the system so agents can clock in and clock out manually while the system verifies their location. Employees can only register attendance when they are within a 150-meter radius of the company. Agents can also manage their leave requests, check their remaining leave balance, and request changes to their scheduled leave days based on the company's approval rules. Administrators have access to attendance data, employee management, leave requests, notifications, and reports.

Key Features

Secure authentication for agents and administrators
Location-based Clock In and Clock Out
150-meter company radius verification
Working hours calculation
Real-time attendance status
Leave request management
Remaining leave balance tracking
Notification system
Admin dashboard with reports
Employee and attendance management

Leave Management Rules

I implemented leave-day change rules to maintain a controlled and transparent process:

1stFirst ChangeNo approval needed
2ndSecond ChangeRequires admin approval
3rdThird ChangeNot allowed
